Quantitative research on subnational conflict and peace trends in South Sudan, supporting peacebuilding analysis and decisions about programs.

03 / MACHINE LEARNING2024–26

Forecasting conflict at a global scale

Production machine-learning workflows generating monthly conflict forecasts for approximately 50,000 districts worldwide, used by the UK and German foreign ministries.

More about this work +

My work covered data sourcing, feature engineering, model training, evaluation, and documentation, including NLP and geoparsing of a news corpus containing more than 10 million articles.

04 / CLIMATE & MIGRATION2025–26

How a changing climate moves us

Modeling migration across the United States and Spain to understand how temperature shocks and extreme weather interact with housing, density, and migration networks.

More about this work +

I brought together long-run climate, migration, demographic, and geospatial data, using econometrics and spatial analysis to examine differences across places. The work informed a policy memo for city leaders.

German Marshall Fund · In collaboration with EconAI researchers

Applied economic and energy analysis for the World Bank's Philippines country office, comparing electricity prices and their drivers across peer countries.

World Bank · Philippines
